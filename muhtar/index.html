Claim your block, grow the map.

The world is split into hexagonal blocks. Whoever solves a block's daily puzzle fastest becomes its chief for the day. Challenge the blocks next door; every Monday everyone starts over. No account, no e-mail; your precise location is never stored, only which block you are in.
